# Design system: L'Angolo del Compleanno

Recorded from the shipped build (pages generated by `src/build.py`, `style.css`, `script.js`). Quiet system at the user's request: warm blush base, colour used sparingly, no eyebrow labels, badges or gloss. Since the multi-page version: firm 4px corners everywhere, real photos of the shop's work, page-to-page transitions.

## Pages

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `index.html` (Home) | Hero with the shop photo, "Alcune delle nostre feste" (6 Instagram photos linking to the posts), reviews, short "Passa in negozio o scrivici" block |
| `chi-siamo.html` | "Dietro ogni palloncino c'è Martina", the story (example text, flagged in the legal notes), the illustrated set-up, "Come lavora Martina", two photos, call to action |
| `catalogo.html` | "Palloncini per ogni festa": five occasion cards with photos plus "Matrimoni, anniversari, lauree"; "Cosa realizziamo": six services with photos, an "E inoltre" list, delivery strip, call to action |
| `contatti.html` (Contattaci) | "Vieni a trovarci in Viale Europa" (contacts, hours, map on click), "Componi la tua festa" form (WhatsApp message, no colour picker) |
| `note-legali.html`, `accessibilita.html` | Legal notes and accessibility statement |

Header, footer ("pedice"), icon sprite and mobile bar are shared partials in `src/build.py`; page bodies live in `src/pages/`. Run `python src/build.py` after editing, `python src/serve.py 5520` to preview with the production headers and clean URLs.

## Type

- Display: **Red Hat Display** variable (self-hosted), weight 700 (800 for the wordmark), tracking -0.02/-0.03em.
- Body/UI: **Red Hat Text** variable, 17px / 1.6.
- Scale: home display `clamp(2.6rem … 5.6rem)`, page titles `clamp(2.3rem … 4.3rem)`, h2 `clamp(1.9rem … 3rem)`, h3 1.2rem, lead `clamp(1.08rem … 1.25rem)`.

## Shape

`--r: 4px` on cards, buttons, inputs, choice chips, map, social links, menu button; `--r-sm: 2px` on the "oggi" badge. Only the open/closed status dot stays round. Cards use a 1px `--line` border rather than heavy shadows.

## Components

- **Work tile (home):** square photo, white caption below (title + one line), whole tile links to the Instagram post in a new tab.
- **Occasion card (catalogo):** photo 4:3 (square thumbnail on phones, horizontal card), title, one sentence, "Chiedi un preventivo" link to `contatti?festa=…#preventivo`, which pre-selects the occasion.
- **Service card:** photo 4:3, title, one sentence; "E inoltre" icon list for services without a photo.
- **Reviews:** big 5,0 score in the section head, three quote cards with a rose top rule.
- **Buttons:** 42/48/54px, 4px corners. Primary rose with white text; ink; line. Text links underlined in rose with a sliding arrow.
- **Footer:** four columns (brand + CTA, Pagine, Negozio, Contatti) over ink, demo note and legal links underneath.

## Continuous background

All pages sit on the same blush ground. `[data-ribbons]` draws two satin ribbons (rose left, gold right) along the page margins, built in JS on the real size of `<main>`, starting under each page's opening block; they unroll as you scroll and never enter the text column (verified at 320–1440px).

## Motion

- Between pages: cross-document View Transitions (`@view-transition { navigation: auto }`): header and mobile bar stay still, content fades out and rises in.
- Load: page title rises word by word, then the rest of the opening block.
- Scroll-linked (only where `animation-timeline` is supported): hero photo drift and hero fade on the home; blocks fade up as they enter; photos settle from a slight zoom; the rules above "Come lavora Martina" draw themselves.
- In view: the illustrated set-up in "Chi siamo" assembles once, then bubble and bouquet sway twice and stop (under 5 seconds).
- Hover: photo zoom inside cards, nav underline slides in, arrow nudges.
- Nothing loops forever; `prefers-reduced-motion` disables transitions and animations.

## Assets

- `img/lavori/*.jpg`: 12 photos from the shop's Instagram (own posts only; no people, no third-party photographer credits, no branded characters), 640px, names on panels and cakes blurred.
